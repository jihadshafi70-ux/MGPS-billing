Hospital Website Integration & Operational Workflow

Step 1: Ward Entry
Nurse Logs Start Time

Nurse selects patient group (Neonate, Infant, Ped/Adult) and delivery device (Prong, Facemask, Reservoir) with exact L/min & L/hr.

Step 2: 24h Watchdog
Forward Timer & Alert

If End Time is omitted, timer counts forward continuously and triggers a 24-hour alert to Biomedical Admin & Ward Nurse.

Step 3: Cashier Desk
Isolated Oxygen Payment

Attendant pays oxygen charge at Cashier Room via API link alongside Pharmacy/Lab/Radiology, keeping Bed Rental separate.

Step 4: Biomed Audit
Real-Time Reconciliation

Biomedical Engineer reconciles billed patient litres against central PSA oxygen plant & manifold output (Daily/Monthly/Quarterly).
